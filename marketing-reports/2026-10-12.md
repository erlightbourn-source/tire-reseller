- A guest "Ask the seller" form is built and dark (`GUEST_INQUIRY` flag, PR #55). It is the highest-leverage buyer-side unlock we have, and it is blocked on one ops fact: confirm Upstash is configured in the prod Worker, because the per-address caps only hold with it (BACKLOG #20).
- Recommendation: Evan/Miles confirm Upstash in the prod Worker, then flip the flag behind one approval card. Effort: S once confirmed. Confidence: high that the friction exists; unknown size (no traffic data).
