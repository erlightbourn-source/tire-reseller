# TireTrader — Growth & UX Funnel Review
**Advisory memo · read-only analysis · 2026-08-24**

Scope: homepage, browse, listing detail, seller flows (sell-tires / subscribe / pro / sell), auth,
seller profiles, dashboard, messages, SEO landing pages (sizes / tires / used-tires/[city]),
`lib/content.js`, `lib/safety.js`, `lib/pricing.js`, `lib/seller.js`, and the Founding Seller program
page. Six weeks have passed since the last review (2026-07-06); this is an independent re-read of
the current code, not a diff of the old memo — every claim below was re-verified against the live
files, including the items that overlap with prior findings.

**Business context this review is weighted against:** TireTrader has ~0 real listings/sellers today
— it's a pre-launch, supply-first marketplace targeting Broward County first (`app/founding-seller`,
`lib/cities.js`). Acquisition suggestions below assume a cold-start market, not existing traffic or
inventory: the job right now is converting the *first 25 sellers*, not optimizing a funnel with real
demand yet.

## TOP FINDING — Trust/security: the login page permanently advertises shared demo credentials

**Stage:** Trust (blocks safe launch)
**What & why:** `components/AuthForm.js` (lines 204-208) renders, unconditionally, on every visit to
`/login`:
```jsx
{!isSignup && (
  <div className="mt-4 bg-white/5 px-3 py-2.5 text-xs text-slate-400 ring-1 ring-inset ring-white/10">
    <span className="font-semibold text-slate-300">Demo:</span> demo@tiretrader.test / demo1234
  </div>
)}
```
This isn't a placeholder comment — `prisma/seed.js` line 116 actually creates this account
(`demo@tiretrader.test` / `demo1234`, seeded as a seller with a free first year), and line 323 prints
the same credentials to the seed console log. There's no `NODE_ENV`/env-flag gate anywhere in
`AuthForm.js`. For an internal/staging build this is harmless; for a marketplace that's actively
recruiting real Broward sellers via `/founding-seller` and will soon carry real listings and buyer
messages, a public, unauthenticated, always-visible credential box invites two problems: (1) anyone
can log into a real seeded account and poke around signed-in surfaces before you're ready for that,
and (2) it reads as unfinished/untrustworthy to a real prospect evaluating whether to hand over tire
inventory to list — exactly the wrong first impression for a trust-dependent local marketplace.
**Where:** `components/AuthForm.js` lines 204-208; account origin `prisma/seed.js` line 116.
**Effort:** S · **Confidence:** High

## ACQUISITION

### A1. The primary seller-conversion page never mentions the Founding Seller program
- **Stage:** Acquisition (cold-start, highest strategic leverage right now)
- **What & why:** `/sell-tires` (`app/sell-tires/page.js`) is the page every "Start selling"/"Sell
  tires" CTA across the site points to (`app/page.js` seller-CTA section, dashboard, nav) — it's the
  actual persuasion surface for the exact goal this launch depends on: recruiting real sellers. I
  grepped the file for "founding"/"Founding" — zero matches. The Founding Seller pitch (first 25 lock
  $10/mo for life, permanent badge, white-glove onboarding — `app/founding-seller/page.js`) is TireTrader's
  single strongest cold-start incentive, and it currently only reaches a seller who happens to notice
  the separate `Founding Seller` nav link (`app/layout.js` line 85) or land on a city page
  (`app/used-tires/[city]/page.js` line 117). A seller who clicks the homepage "Start selling" button
  or the dashboard's "Become a seller" link and lands directly on `/sell-tires` sees generic
  "$10/month after year one" messaging with no reason to act *now* vs. later — undercutting the whole
  "first 25" urgency mechanic. Add a founding-cohort banner/line to the hero or pricing card on
  `/sell-tires` linking to `/founding-seller`.
- **Where:** `app/sell-tires/page.js` (hero section lines 37-64, pricing card lines 107-130).
- **Effort:** S · **Confidence:** High

## TRUST

### T1. Pro upgrade page's "Demo: simulated" disclaimer isn't gated the way the subscribe page's is
- **Stage:** Trust → Conversion
- **What & why:** `app/subscribe/page.js` correctly checks `stripeConfigured()` (line 26) and only
  shows dev-mode language when Stripe keys are absent (line 92: `live ? "Secure checkout via Stripe
  (test mode)" : "⚙️ Dev mode: checkout is simulated..."`). `app/pro/page.js` line 57 renders
  `"⚙️ Demo: upgrade is simulated. Real billing uses Stripe."` as static, unconditional text — it
  never imports or checks `stripeConfigured()` at all, and `components/ProButton.js` posts straight to
  `/api/pro` with no live/demo branching visible to the user. Two problems: (1) it's inconsistent with
  the pattern the team already built and uses correctly one page over, and (2) if Stripe billing goes
  live for the seller tier but the Pro page isn't updated in lockstep, this $25/mo upsell page will
  keep telling paying prospects their upgrade is fake — a very avoidable, self-inflicted trust hit on
  the product's highest-priced tier.
- **Where:** `app/pro/page.js` line 57 (compare pattern in `app/subscribe/page.js` lines 26, 92);
  `lib/stripe.js` `stripeConfigured()`.
- **Effort:** S · **Confidence:** High

### T2. Pricing copy conflicts between the Founding Seller page and every other seller-pricing surface
- **Stage:** Trust → Conversion
- **What & why:** `app/founding-seller/page.js` states: *"The first 25 Founding Sellers lock in $10/mo
  for life after that; **every seller after pays $25/mo**."* But `app/sell-tires/page.js` ("$10/month
  after year one · cancel anytime," line 57 and pricing card line 114) and `app/subscribe/page.js`
  ("then $10/month after {freeEnds} · cancel anytime," line 58) both market the *standard* plan as a
  flat $10/mo with no mention of a future increase. $25/mo is the price of the separate, optional Pro
  add-on everywhere else in the app (`app/pro/page.js`, `lib/content.js`) — so a seller reading
  `/founding-seller` reasonably concludes the *base* plan itself is going up to $25/mo once the
  founding cohort fills, while every other page they'd read tells them $10/mo, indefinitely. I'm not
  suggesting a pricing change — only flagging that the *communication* is internally inconsistent and
  should say the same thing everywhere (or make explicit that $25/mo there means "the Pro upsell," not
  the base plan).
- **Where:** `app/founding-seller/page.js` (PERKS array item 3 + intro paragraph), `app/sell-tires/page.js`,
  `app/subscribe/page.js`.
- **Effort:** S (copy-only clarification) · **Confidence:** Med-High

### T3. "Verified Pro sellers" language spans four surfaces; Pro has no actual verification step (recurring)
- **Stage:** Trust
- **What & why:** Re-verified independently this pass and it's broader than the 7/6 finding suggested:
  the word "verified" attached to Pro sellers now appears in **four** separate places — the homepage
  TRUST bullet (`app/page.js` line 23: "Verified Pro sellers — Ratings, reviews, and trusted-seller
  badges you can vet"), `SELLER_BENEFITS` in `lib/content.js` (line 49: "Verified Pro badge — Stand out
  with a trusted seller badge"), `BUYER_FAQ` (`lib/content.js` line 19: "Verified Pro sellers have an
  active subscription and a track record"), and `app/founding-seller/page.js` (line 20: "a verified
  badge — normally a $25/mo upgrade"). But `lib/seller.js`'s `isProSeller()` is `!!(s.pro ||
  s.foundingSeller)` — a pure billing flag — and `app/pro/page.js`'s PERKS list (priority placement,
  badge, bulk tool, unlimited featured, priority support) contains no identity/inventory verification
  step at all. If a scammer just pays $25/mo, the product's own marketing copy tells buyers they're
  "verified" and "trusted" — that's a real liability if anyone gets burned by a bad-faith Pro seller
  and points back at this language. Cheapest fix: soften the word to "Pro sellers" / "Priority-placed
  sellers" across all four surfaces; real fix (a genuine verification step) is a bigger product call.
- **Where:** `app/page.js` line 23, `lib/content.js` lines 19 & 49, `app/founding-seller/page.js` line 20.
- **Effort:** S (copy) / L (real verification) · **Confidence:** High

### T4. Seller "usually responds within a day" is a hardcoded label, not a measured signal
- **Stage:** Trust → Conversion
- **What & why:** `app/listings/[id]/page.js` line 237 renders `⚡ Usually responds within a day`
  for any seller where `isProSeller()` is true — it's not computed from `Message`/`Thread` timestamps
  anywhere in the codebase (no query for it in this file or `lib/`). It's decorative today, but it
  will become an actively false claim the moment a Pro seller stops replying to messages, and buyers
  have no way to tell the difference between a responsive seller and one who simply paid for Pro. A
  real "avg reply: N hours" computed from `thread.messages` timestamps would be both more credible and
  a genuine incentive for sellers to stay responsive — worth it as reply-speed is one of the few signals
  a two-sided marketplace can earn honestly at launch with near-zero listings.
- **Where:** `app/listings/[id]/page.js` line 237; would also apply to `app/sellers/[id]/page.js`.
- **Effort:** M · **Confidence:** Med

## CONVERSION

### C1. Seller profile has no way to message the seller; listing cards don't link back to the profile
- **Stage:** Conversion
- **What & why:** `app/sellers/[id]/page.js` shows ratings, review text, sold count, and member-since
  (lines 45-69) — real social proof — but the only interactive elements on the page are `BlockSeller`
  and `ReviewForm`; there is no `MessageSeller` import or any "message this seller" affordance, so a
  buyer convinced by the reviews has to navigate back into one of the seller's listings to act on that
  trust. In the other direction, `components/ListingCard.js` wraps the entire card in a single `Link`
  to `/listings/[id]` (line 19) with no secondary link to the seller — so a buyer scanning `/browse`
  never discovers the profile/reviews at all unless they open a listing first. Both ends of this
  cross-link are still missing; closing either one connects the review social proof to an actual next
  action.
- **Where:** `app/sellers/[id]/page.js`, `components/ListingCard.js`.
- **Effort:** S · **Confidence:** Med

### C2. The "fair price" comp claim is never clickable — it's an assertion, not proof
- **Stage:** Conversion
- **What & why:** `lib/pricing.js` computes a solid signal (`"X% below the typical price... avg $Y
  (N listings)"`), and it renders on both the listing card (`components/ListingCard.js` lines 87-90,
  just the delta %) and the detail page (`app/listings/[id]/page.js` lines 195-204, the full "avg
  $X/tire (N listings)" text). In neither place is the "(N listings)" text a link to those actual
  comps — a skeptical used-tire buyer (per the trust-first framing this category needs) has to take
  the number on faith. Making "(N listings)" navigate to a pre-filtered `/browse?size=...` view turns
  a claim into something the buyer can independently verify, which is exactly the kind of proof this
  category's buyers are looking for before they message a stranger.
- **Where:** `components/ListingCard.js` lines 87-90; `app/listings/[id]/page.js` lines 195-204;
  `lib/pricing.js`.
- **Effort:** S-M · **Confidence:** Med

## ACTIVATION

### V1. Pro sellers get no nudge from the single-listing form toward bulk-add
- **Stage:** Activation
- **What & why:** `app/sell/page.js` has zero mentions of "bulk" (grepped directly) despite
  `app/sell/bulk/` existing as a real route and being a named Pro perk on both `/pro`
  ("📥 Bulk listing tool") and the dashboard (`app/dashboard/page.js` line 96: "📥 Bulk add" — but only
  as a top-of-page button, not inside the listing form itself). A Pro seller who opens `/sell` to add
  their 4th or 5th tire set one-by-one gets no in-context "listing more than one set? Use bulk add"
  prompt at the exact moment it would save them the most time — the tool they're paying $25/mo partly
  for is invisible from the workflow it's meant to replace.
- **Where:** `app/sell/page.js`.
- **Effort:** S · **Confidence:** Med

## Top 3 to do next

1. **Trust/security — remove or gate the always-visible demo login credentials.** `components/AuthForm.js`
   lines 204-208. Cheapest, highest-confidence item in the memo, and the one most likely to embarrass
   the product in front of a real Broward seller prospect if it ships to them as-is.
2. **A1 — Put the Founding Seller pitch on `/sell-tires`.** The whole growth strategy right now is
   "recruit the first 25 sellers," and the page built to convert sellers doesn't mention the offer
   built to convert them.
3. **T3 — Stop calling Pro "verified" across all four copy surfaces.** Same fix, one meaning, applied
   consistently (`app/page.js`, `lib/content.js` ×2, `app/founding-seller/page.js`) — cheap, and it
   closes a real "burned by a Verified seller" liability before real money changes hands.

**Needs Evan (product/business decision first):**

  timestamps, decide the computation window and fallback for sellers with no reply history yet) —
  buildable by the data-engineer once scoped, but the scoping itself (what counts as a "reply," what's
  shown before a seller has any message history) is worth a quick product call.
