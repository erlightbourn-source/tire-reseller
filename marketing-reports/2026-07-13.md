# TireTrader — Weekly UX / Growth Review — 2026-07-13

**Reviewer:** Dev's Monday marketing/UX pass (independent `marketing` consultant subagent; advisory only — no code, schema, content, or monetization changed).
**Scope:** live App Router code — homepage `app/page.js`, `app/browse`, `app/listings/[id]`, seller flows (`app/sell-tires`/`app/subscribe`/`app/pro`/`BecomeSeller.js`), auth (`AuthForm.js`), seller profiles `app/sellers/[id]`, SEO hubs `app/sizes`/`app/tires`, copy in `lib/content.js`, and supporting components.
**Constraints respected:** brutalist identity (black + acid-yellow `#e5ff00`, Courier/Georgia, square edges) and the fixed monetization (browse free; sellers free year one, then $10/mo; $25/mo Pro) — every item lives inside them.

**Overall:** a well-built, honest marketplace with genuinely strong trust scaffolding already (fair-price context, DOT-age flags, off-platform scam detection, buyer checklist at point-of-pay). The highest-leverage gaps: (1) a buyer-activation gate heavier than it needs to be, (2) a "Verified" trust claim the product doesn't actually back, and (3) seller copy that leads with the price instead of the free year.

---

## Ranked suggestions (funnel leverage: acquisition → activation → conversion → retention → trust)

### 1. Buyer messaging gate is the funnel's biggest drop — and it loses the drafted message
**Stage: Activation · Effort: M · Confidence: High**
"Message seller" is the core buyer conversion event, but `components/MessageSeller.js:14-21` hard-blocks it behind login, and `components/AuthForm.js:50-56` creates new accounts unverified with no session ("check your email"). A first-time buyer faces: signup → open email → click verify → log in → land back on the listing → re-open the collapsed composer → retype their question → send. The pre-filled draft ("Hi! Is this set still available?", `MessageSeller.js:10`) is discarded across the round trip. Worst for cold-start visitors — exactly the audience the Founding Seller promo will drive in.
**Safe slice:** persist the drafted message (localStorage keyed by listing) + auto-reopen the composer pre-filled on return, so the trip ends on a one-tap send. **Bigger swing (product decision):** let buyers compose first, collapse auth into the send action.

### 2. "Verified Pro sellers" overstates what the product verifies — trust-wedge risk
**Stage: Trust · Effort: S (copy) / L (real signal) · Confidence: High**
The homepage trust grid promises "Verified Pro sellers" (`app/page.js:20`) and the seller page lists a "Verified Pro badge" (`app/sell-tires/page.js:119`), but `PRO` is purely a paid-tier flag set by the Stripe webhook (`app/api/pro/route.js:6`) — there is no identity, condition, or inspection verification (the only "verify" in the product is email confirmation; `lib/legal.js:8` states "We don't inspect… or guarantee any tires"). Used-tire trust is the stated wedge, so a "Verified" label meaning "has a card on file" is both a credibility risk and a missed differentiator.
**Near-term:** soften copy to what's true ("Pro sellers" / "Rated & reviewed sellers"). **Real leverage:** attach the Founding Seller promo to an actual verification standard (required DOT-stamp + tread photo, or a condition attestation) and let *that* earn the badge — turning the promo into the trust mechanic the wedge needs.

### 3. Seller CTAs lead with "$10/month" and bury the free first year
**Stage: Conversion · Effort: S · Confidence: High**
The strongest seller hook is "first year free," but the primary homepage seller banner headline reads "Sell tire inventory for $10/month" (`app/page.js:167`), with "First year free" demoted to a mid-paragraph aside (`:170`). The pricing card leads "$0 for year one" correctly (`app/sell-tires.js:113`) — so messaging is internally inconsistent, and the most-seen surface leads with the cost.
**Fix (copy only, `app/page.js`):** flip the banner headline to lead with the free year ("List your tire inventory free for a year"), keep "$10/mo after, cancel anytime" as the sub.

### 4. Homepage promises "make offers" but the buy box has no offer entry point
**Stage: Conversion · Effort: M · Confidence: Med**
`app/page.js:18` and `MessageSeller.js:17` tell buyers they can "make an offer," and an offers system exists (`app/api/offers/route.js`, surfaced in `ChatWindow.js`) — but the listing buy box only exposes a plain "Message seller" composer (`app/listings/[id]/page.js:252`). Buyers can't make a structured price offer until deep in a thread. A "Make an offer" affordance in the buy box (pre-filling an amount vs. the listed price) matches the promise and gives price-sensitive buyers a lower-commitment action than a cold message.

### 5. SEO size/brand hubs have no browsable index or internal-linking spine
**Stage: Acquisition · Effort: M · Confidence: Med**
`/sizes/[size]` and `/tires/[brand]` are solid, canonicalized, JSON-LD'd landing pages — real acquisition assets. But interlinking is thin: the homepage "Shop by brand" only lists brands with active inventory (`app/page.js:181-196`), and there's no index hub for popular sizes / all brands. A `/sizes` and `/tires` index (popular-sizes grid + full brand list), linked from footer + homepage, deepens internal linking and gives the pages a browsable entry — compounding once the Founding Seller promo seeds inventory.

### 6. Seller path to first listing is a 5-hop corridor even though it's $0/no-card
**Stage: Activation · Effort: M · Confidence: Med**
Becoming a listing seller runs signup → email verify → login → `/subscribe` interstitial → "Start selling free" → `/dashboard` → `/sell` → form. The `/subscribe` step takes no payment and no card (`BecomeSeller.js:31-38` just flips the flag) — a pure interstitial. For `role=seller` signups, flipping the free-seller flag at account creation and routing straight to `/sell` collapses two hops. (Product decision: whether you want the explicit plan-consent moment.)

### 7. Dev/demo language and demo credentials leak to end users — launch-readiness
**Stage: Trust · Effort: S · Confidence: High**
The login form renders `Demo: demo@tiretrader.test / demo1234` unconditionally on the login tab (`AuthForm.js:205-207`, gated only by `!isSignup`), and `/pro` shows "⚙️ Demo: upgrade is simulated" (`app/pro/page.js:57`). On a real launch these read as unfinished and undercut trust at the moment a seller decides to pay. Gate them behind an env flag so production is clean — **but keep the flag ON through the current demo/preview phase** so Evan's live demo access isn't removed prematurely.

### 8. Hardcoded "Usually responds within a day" isn't measured
**Stage: Trust · Effort: S · Confidence: Med**
The seller trust card shows "⚡ Usually responds within a day" for any Pro seller (`app/listings/[id]/page.js:234`) — a static string, not a computed response time. Same integrity family as #2. Either compute it from thread reply latency or drop it.

### 9. "Safe install hand-off" trust angle is unaddressed
**Stage: Trust · Effort: M · Confidence: Med**
Buyer guidance is strong on inspection (`app/listings/[id]/page.js:318-347`, `lib/content.js` checklist) and off-platform safety (`lib/safety.js`), but the "meet safely / install hand-off" piece stops at "meet in a public place." For used tires, "meet at an installer and pay after they mount/inspect" is a concrete trust upgrade and a natural Founding-Seller talking point.

## Top 3 to do next

1. **Preserve the drafted message across the login round trip (#1).** *Engineer-safe:* yes — localStorage draft + auto-reopen composer; no schema, no billing. Highest activation leverage for least risk. (The deeper "compose before auth" rework is a separate product decision.)
2. **Fix the "Verified" copy now; design the real verification signal next (#2).** *Split:* the copy correction (`app/page.js:20`, `app/sell-tires/page.js:119`) is engineer-safe and removes the overstatement immediately. The actual verification standard tied to the Founding Seller promo **needs a product/design decision** (what's verified, what earns the badge).
3. **Lead the homepage seller banner with the free year (#3).** *Engineer-safe:* yes — copy-only in `app/page.js:167-170`.

## Strengths worth preserving (don't refactor away)
No-account email-demand capture (`EmailAlertForm`), fair-price cohort context on cards and buy box, DOT-age aging flags, and the off-platform scam detector — already doing real trust/retention work, ahead of typical marketplace MVPs.
