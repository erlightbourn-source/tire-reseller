  reachable regardless of UI. Added `lib/image.js#stripDataUriMetadata` (decodes the
  base64 payload, dispatches through the existing `stripMetadata`, re-encodes), wired
  into both routes' photo arrays.
  **Measurable effect:** 4 new tests (GPS-tagged PNG data URI stripped and still decodes
  as valid PNG; `image/jpg`→`image/jpeg` MIME normalization + APP1/Exif removal;
  non-data-URI and malformed inputs pass through byte-identical) — suite 90→94, all green.
  Auto-fix category per policy (localized, behavior-preserving, PII-leak class matching
  an already-approved fix pattern) — not auth-flow, billing, schema, or destructive.
